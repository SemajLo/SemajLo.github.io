Flagship project · side-built, in active use

Global Pricing Intelligence.

One question, answered for 175 markets: what should a subscription cost here, so it's fair to a local family and sound for the business?

175markets priced against PPP & GDP benchmarks
18,800+Apple & Google price points imported and indexed
10 min → secper price lookup, vs. drilling through store consoles

The problem. Apple and Google set international prices by exchange rate, not affordability — so a US-anchored price lands very differently in Oslo than in Jakarta. And just checking one price meant five levels of store-console screens and an unsearchable region list.

What I built. A PPP-led pricing model with GDP per capita as a secondary signal, Apple and Google treated independently, recommendations snapped to valid platform price points, competitor pricing in view, and a transparent calculation chain — because a tool nobody can audit is a tool nobody trusts.

The result. A shared reference for Product, UA, Marketing and Business. Guidance, never auto-changes: pricing stays a human decision — the tool makes it an informed one.
