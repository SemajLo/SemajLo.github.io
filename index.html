Global Pricing Intelligence Dashboard

A decision-support tool that answers one question for 175 markets: what should this subscription cost here, so it's fair to a local family and sound for the business?

The problem

Apple and Google set international prices by exchange rate, not by what people can afford — so a US-anchored $12.99 subscription lands very differently in Oslo than in Jakarta. Nobody on the team could see where the gaps were.

Worse, even checking a single price was painful: log in to the store console, pick the app, drill through five levels of subscription screens, then scroll an unsearchable region list. Roughly ten minutes per product per region — hours across apps, for every team that needed a number.

What I built

  • PPP-led pricing model — purchasing-power-parity conversion as the primary signal, GDP per capita as a secondary affordability check, per market.
  • Apple and Google treated independently, with recommended prices snapped to each platform's valid price points.
  • Instant search and cross-product comparison — any price, any market, any product, in seconds.
  • Transparent calculation chain — every recommendation shows its working, because a tool nobody can audit is a tool nobody trusts.
  • Competitor pricing view, so price points are set with the market in sight, not in a vacuum.

The recommendation engine

Each market is scored against configurable affordability thresholds and sorted into an action band — guidance, never auto-changes. Pricing stays a human decision; the tool makes it an informed one.

ReduceOverpriced >20% vs. local purchasing power
ReviewDrifting 10–20% out of alignment
MaintainWithin ±10% — aligned
InvestigateUnderpriced — possible revenue left behind

Impact

Before~10 min per price lookup, store console only
AfterSeconds — searchable, comparable, explained

Now a shared reference for Product, UA, Marketing and Business: anyone can gauge a sensible price point for a region without touching a store console. Roadmap: live App Store Connect / Play Console pricing, performance data (trials, conversion, ARPU, churn) and an AI recommendation layer.

Why it's credible: I designed the model, defined the thresholds, and built the tool — the same end-to-end ownership I bring to any product problem.
